🌞 5 daily basics that help everything

  • Sleep on time — aim to sleep before 10 pm and wake up early.
  • Eat warm, freshly cooked food at roughly the same times every day.
  • Move every day — a 30-minute walk is enough.
  • Oil massage — a quick warm-oil massage before bath keeps skin, joints and sleep healthy.
  • Breathe — 10 minutes of slow, deep breathing calms the mind and the body.
